# Stakeholders
- Student: Finding and booking equipment
- Lecturer: Getting equipment when needed for teaching
- Equipment Technician: Managing equipment and returns
- Department Administrator: Managing bookings and records
- Equipment Manager: Control, availability and accountability

# Stakeholder Needs and Concerns;
- Student: This stakeholder needs a method of finding certain equipment items, when they are available, whether they are permitted to book them, and to be able to book them. A student may be concerned about what equipment they are permitted to book.
- Lecturer: This stakeholder has similar needs to a student. This stakeholder may be concerned about equipment not being available when it is needed.
- Equipment Technician: This stakeholder needs a system that permits logging of the status of each equipment item, and logging returns of said equipment items. An Equipment Technician may be concerned about equipment not being returned on time.
- Department Administrator: This stakeholder needs a system that has records of equipment bookings and is capable of managing different bookings. A Department Administrator may be concerned about what information is contained in records.
- Equipment Manager: This stakeholder needs a system that allows them to log availability of different equipment pieces, and who to hold accountable if equipment is returned in a state deemed unsatisfactory. This stakeholder may be concerned about how much control they have over the equipment.

# Interview Notes
## Equipment Manager
1. We see if the equipment is returned on time, and if any damages are present that were originally undocumented.
2. Late returns impose a temporary ban on future bookings, depending on the severity and regularity of the offense.
3. Damaged equipment items impose a temporary ban on future bookings, and depending on the severity, the offender is fined.
4. Lecturers may book any equipment items, whereas Students may only book equipment depending on their course, such as an art student being able to book a camera but not a laptop.
5. If there is a no-show we attempt to make contact with the booker, if there is no response, the equipment item is made available for other bookings. 
## Department Manager
1. If a booking is no longer wanted, we free up the equipment for bookings at the cancelled time.
2. We take a name, phone number, and email.
3. Various spreadsheets.
4. As previous, various spreadsheets.
# Candidate Requirement
## What Was Learned?
1. There are punishments to late and/or damaged returns.
2. Records and bookings are kept in various spreadsheets rather than a centralized system.
3. Different stakeholders have different booking eligibility.
## Requirement
The system shall have allow for users to book specific equipment at specific times depending on the eligibility of the user.
