# Week 1 -- Initial Discovery
## 1. Facts
Bookings are managed through a mixture of emails, spreadsheets, and informal arrangements.
Equipment can be double booked.
People are unsure whether equipment is available.
Staff spend time checking bookings manually.
Equipment may not be returned when expected.
## 2. Assumptions
We want to know how much equipment is available in total.
We would want to know what equipment is available at different times
Different equipment pieces have different time limits.
We want details of the person booking the equipment.
Staff would have priority over students.
Bookings can be cancelled/modified.
## 3. Unknowns
Should the system be manual or automated?
What should be done if equipment is not returned?
How much time should be allotted for a booking?
How is the equipment being supplied?
Who can book equipment?
## 4. Stakeholders
Students: Availability, easy booking.
Staff: Lesson planning.
Technicians: Maintenance time.
Admin: Do they need to purchase more equipment?
Security: Tracing whereabouts of equipment and whether it is missing and/or has been stolen.

Staff and Students may run into issues with Technicians as if equipment is booked without break, there is little to no time for maintenance. Staff and Students may also have conflict with Admin as there may not be enough equipment for everyone.
## 5. Goals
The system should centralize where bookings for different equipment pieces are made.
The system should make it easy for staff and students to book different equipment pieces.
The system should help the college to study the demand of different equipment pieces.
## 6. Scope
# In Scope
There is no concrete system for making a booking.
Human error can cause confusion and difficulty in booking.
There may be preferential treatment involved in booking.
# Out of Scope / Not Yet Established
Staff are able to override a students booking.
Punishments for late/damaged equipment.
Method of dealing with no-shows.
## 7. Candidate Requirements
The system shall allow authorized users to make a booking for specific equipment at a specific time & date.
The system shall allow staff to observe who has booked equipment, when the equipment was booked, and where it will be going.
## 8. Requirement Surgery
R1: The system should be easy to use.
'Easy to use' does not specify what parts of the system should be easy to use.

R1 Rewritten: The system should have an easily understandable and clearly labelled User Interface so that users will not encounter confusion when attempting to use it.

R2: The system should be secure.
Secure is a very loose term that does not cover what parts of the system are vulnerable.

R2 Rewritten: The system should only permit authorized users to make bookings, edit bookings, and to access an Admin system.
